Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JOKERS J&Y and the Physics Video Factory ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible via toumawu.de, collect, use, and manage your data when connecting with social media services, including the TikTok for Developers API.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account with Physics Video Factory via TikTok Login Kit, we may receive:

  • Basic public profile information (such as your TikTok username, display nickname, and avatar image) under the user.info.basic permission scope.
  • OAuth 2.0 access credentials needed strictly to authenticate video upload requests authorized by you.

We do NOT collect, harvest, or sell any personal data of external TikTok viewers.

2. How We Use the Information

We use the authorized TikTok API scopes exclusively to:

  • Identify and verify the authorized account owner (user.info.basic).
  • Publish and upload educational and entertainment 2D physics simulation videos directly to your TikTok account as posts or drafts (video.publish and video.upload).

3. Data Storage & Security

All authentication tokens are stored securely and locally on your own managed server environment. We do not sell, rent, or transfer your tokens to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

You can revoke Physics Video Factory's access to your TikTok account at any time either through your TikTok Mobile App settings (under Security > Manage app permissions) or by clicking "Disconnect" inside the application interface.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@toumawu.de
